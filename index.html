Sector assignments

Flight Command (Mi-8 Pilots)

The primary tactical insertion and logistics delivery arm of the squadron. Responsible for operating the group's central transport fleet.

  • Responsibilities: Execute precision low-altitude flight paths, hot-zone troop insertions, and synchronized material resupply missions under heavy observation.
  • Requirements: Flawless flight mechanics control, steady landing profiles, and absolute mastery of multi-channel command net coordination.

Enlisted Flight Crew (Crew Chiefs & Aerial Observers)

Crucial flight deck positions tasked with airframe oversight, target tracking, and internal cargo management. Experience utilizing USMC or similar maritime air-wing structural frameworks is highly valued.

  • Crew Chiefs & Aerial Observers: In charge of in-flight mechanics, landing clearing commands for the pilot, door-gun suppression coordination, and handling hot logistical loading.

Aviation Maintenance Crew (Airframers, Powerplant, & Avionics)

The technical core tasked with the structural maintenance, system configurations, and back-end logistical health of the squadron's fleet.

  • Experienced Airframers: Specialized in structural integrity, vehicle repair priority routing, and resource management at forward outposts.
  • Powerplant Mechanics: Dedicated to turbine/engine operational maintenance, fuel network logistics, and heavy deployment construction efficiency.
  • Avionics Men: Tasked with communication array monitoring, navigation planning support, and coordinate defense perimeter hardware setups.

Tactical Field Operations (Firearm Experience / Veterans)

The frontline tactical detachment responsible for securing landing zones, managing defensive boundaries, and direct sector containment.

Squadron protocols

  • Presence mandatory for weekly tactical deployments and flight checkrides.
  • Adherence to flight mechanics, communication formats, and mission dispatch paths.
